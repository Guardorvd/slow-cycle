# Tests — integrity and authority

- Exercise real production logic; mocks may isolate justified external boundaries, not replace the math/domain under test. No test-specific production hooks, hardcoded fixture responses, assertion dilution, silent skips/todo/suppression, type bypass or fabricated results.
- Existing test code, helpers, assertions, thresholds, coverage and mandatory gates are protected. Any mutation/reclassification, including syntax-helper fixes, requires explicit human approval of the specific change. General feature approval is insufficient; new test/negative-fixture scope must be explicit in the approved task whitelist.
- A test is not automatic authority over the target product. Record legacy expectation vs approved target conflicts with owner, requirement, source and evidence; preserve current regression protection/gates until human resolution. Do not silently change tests or force future regional architecture back into legacy DAG semantics.
- D1 classifies no existing suite. Category definitions live in [Test Strategy](../../docs/TEST_STRATEGY.md); assignment/TEST_MATRIX belongs to separately approved Q0. Do not label a suite historical to avoid acceptance.
- Read [frozen test-integrity](../../.antigravity/rules/test-integrity.md). Its anti-gaming/escalation protection remains; root authority and the specific human mutation gate apply. An independently always-applied tool rule conflict is escalated, not silently overridden.
- Distinguish expected from actual assertions, coverage and completion. Check full logs, aborts/timeouts and unexpected engine errors/leaks. Known baseline failures are not waivers; exit 0 or an expected assertion budget is insufficient evidence.
- A negative fixture must exercise the real path and produce the expected reason; an arbitrary crash/timeout is not negative PASS. Missing required fixtures mean INCOMPLETE; wrong acceptance/reason means FAIL. Do not suppress expected-negative logs; match them to the declared reason.
- Real physics means registered collision and Input-driven player interaction; teleport/provided mesh/geometry replay do not establish ride coverage. Vulkan evidence needs actual saved/read captures with context. E7 belongs to a human.
- Verify/review do not fix implementation or tests. Return findings to the implementation owner; after fixes require fresh verification/review of the new source/diff identity's impact set ([Test Strategy §7](../../docs/TEST_STRATEGY.md)).
