## 1. Accepted outcome

Every `MOUNTAIN_RIVER_VALLEY` region now has a deterministic, terrain-coherent hydrology built on top of R1 geography and R2 base terrain:

- **Major river** derived from the R1 valley: valley axis + slope-capped seeded meander inside the R1 floor (40 m bank margin), edge to edge along the valley's downstream direction; water surface = monotone fit of the base heights minus a bank, **strictly decreasing** (≥ 1 cm per station); width 12–60 m from drainage area (upstream inflow + in-region accumulation).
- **Drainage** for every point of the region: priority-flood routing on a 32 m sub-lattice of TerrainField heights (plus floodplain shaping), D8 receivers without crossings; every point ends at the major river, a permitted region-edge outlet or a recorded CLOSED body.
- **Channels:** tributaries and creeks as Horton main stems with side tributaries, Strahler order, TRIBUTARY/CREEK and perennial classes, strictly decreasing water surfaces, mouths on their parents.
- **Water bodies:** ponds/lakes with outlets cut ≤ 10 m (basin outlets) and forced outlet creeks; depressions deeper than 18 m recorded as CLOSED wetlands.
- **Hydrology deformation** (the R7 FinalSurface addend): floodplain shaping, river bed/banks, creek gullies; bounded (±15 m), continuous, zero outside floor margin and channel corridors. `TerrainField` is unchanged as base terrain; the transitional `HydrologySurface` composes base + deformation.
- **Seed variation:** a hydrology character (wetness, river style, floodplain wetness, upstream size) from a new registered `hydrology` seed, combined with R1 layout and flow direction; 240 regions → 240 distinct plans, 4–20 river tributaries.
- Inspection: opt-in hydrology mode in `region_preview` (diagnostic water), Vulkan capture script, headless 2D map/sweep tool.

R0/R1/R2 behaviour unchanged: their suites keep stdout identical to the R2 record and the 9 default captures are byte-identical to R1/R2. ## 3. Target behaviour — recommended model: structure-guided drainage

```text
RegionGenerator.build → RegionPlan ──┐                                  (R0/R1, unchanged)
TerrainField.create(RegionPlan) ─────┤                                  (R2, unchanged numerics)
                                     ▼
HydrologyGenerator.build(region_plan, terrain_field) → HydrologyPlan    (R3: owner of hydrological truth)
HydrologyField.create(hydrology_plan, terrain_field)  → water / proximity / drainage / deformation queries
HydrologySurface.create(terrain_field, hydrology_field) → base + hydrology deformation, TerrainField-shaped API
TerrainTileRenderer (accepts TerrainField or HydrologySurface) → region_preview (opt-in hydrology mode)
```

Three tiers, simplest that is coherent; no erosion or watershed simulation:

1. **Major river — structural.** Derived from the R1 valley, not placed afterwards: its centreline is the valley axis plus a seeded meander that stays inside the R1 floor (tighter in narrows, freer in the floodplain basin), entering at the upstream region edge (u = 0) and leaving at the downstream edge (u = 4096). Its water surface is a **monotone non-increasing profile fitted to TerrainField heights** along the centreline; bed = surface − depth. Width/depth grow with drainage area (seeded upstream inflow from beyond the region + in-region accumulation). The R1 floodplain basin becomes the wet/open floodplain reach.
2. **Drainage routing — terrain-driven.** A 32 m analysis lattice (sub-lattice of TerrainField's 16 m lattice, heights via TerrainField queries only). Outlets: the river corridor (receives water at a river station) and the region boundary. Deterministic priority-flood with a total order (height, lattice index) gives every lattice point exactly one receiver and one outlet, and contributing (drainage) area. Saddles and crests become divides naturally; side valleys between spurs concentrate flow. Closed depressions: small ones are resolved by the routing and breached in channel profiles; ones above area/depth thresholds become recorded water bodies (e.g. a tarn or wetland in the R1 upland basin) with a spill outlet or explicit `CLOSED` status — never silently dropped.
3. **Channel network.** Channel heads where drainage area exceeds a seeded initiation threshold; channels follow receivers to the river (confluence at a recorded river station) or the region edge; Strahler order; classes MAJOR_RIVER / TRIBUTARY / CREEK and perennial/intermittent; smoothed polylines that never cross except at confluences; monotone water profiles.

**Hydrology deformation** (the R7 FinalSurface addend `Hydrology deformation`, owned by hydrology): river trough + banks + floodplain levelling toward the river surface inside the floor corridor; creek incision (gully/ravine) scaled by order, tapering to zero at heads and blending into the parent at mouths; water bodies level. Bounded (|Δ| ≤ declared maximum), continuous, exactly zero outside declared influence corridors. TerrainField is **not** modified; `HydrologySurface` composes base + deformation for inspection and for pre-R7 consumers, and is absorbed by FinalSurface in R7.

**Seed variation.** A hydrology *character* is drawn first from a new `hydrology` seed (compositional, like R1): wetness (creek density, perennial share), river style (sinuosity, meander wavelength, side bias), floodplain wetness extent, upstream river size. It combines with terrain-inherited variation (spur/side-valley layout → which side valleys carry water, tributary count/hierarchy and confluence positions; range/far forms; upland basin presence; flow direction from frame symmetry). Per-catchment choices are keyed by stable lattice identity, not discovery order.

**Exclusions:** biomes/moisture fields, rideability, routes/roads, crossings/bridges/fords, vegetation, FinalSurface, streaming/LOD/threading, cross-region river continuity, erosion/sediment simulation, braided channels/oxbows, sophisticated water rendering, player/camera/physics, main-game wiring, any gameplay mechanic, any R1 geography change.

**HydrologyGenerator** `static build(region_plan, terrain_field) -> {is_valid, plan, reason_code, detail}` — `ERR_HYDRO_INPUT_MISSING`; `ERR_HYDRO_INPUT_INVALID` (invalid region, or field signature ≠ region signature); `ERR_HYDRO_ARCHETYPE`; `ERR_HYDRO_CONSTRUCTION` (a construction step failed, with diagnostic detail); `ERR_HYDRO_PLAN_INVALID` (+ validate reasons). **HydrologyPlan** (`slow_cycle.hydrology/1`, RefCounted, immutable, integer-quantised storage like R1 so `canonical_text`/`signature` are exact): input identity (region signature, TerrainField schema tag, hydrology seed); character; channels (id, class, perennial flag, Strahler order, parent id + mouth station or outlet kind, polyline in world metres with per-vertex station, water surface, bed, width, drainage area); water bodies (kind, outline, level, outlet channel or `CLOSED`); floodplain reaches (station ranges on the major river); upstream inflow area; 32 m drainage lattice (receiver/outlet/catchment id + area, compact packed form). `validate()` → fixed-order reasons: `ERR_HYDRO_SCHEMA`, `ERR_HYDRO_MAJOR_RIVER` (not exactly one, or not edge-to-edge), `ERR_HYDRO_TOPOLOGY` (cycle, orphan, multiple parents, mouth not on parent), `ERR_HYDRO_MONOTONIC` (water surface rises downstream, bed above surface), `ERR_HYDRO_HIERARCHY` (area/order not consistent downstream), `ERR_HYDRO_BOUNDS` (outside region, non-finite, width/depth out of range), `ERR_HYDRO_GEOMETRY` (degenerate segment, self/mutual crossing outside confluences), `ERR_HYDRO_DRAINAGE` (lattice point without an outlet outside a recorded closed water body). Diagnostic only, never repairs.

**HydrologyField** `static create(hydrology_plan, terrain_field)` — `ERR_HYDRO_PLAN_MISSING`, `ERR_HYDRO_PLAN_INVALID`, `ERR_HYDRO_TERRAIN_MISMATCH`. World-space queries on the closed region square (`ERR_HYDRO_NONFINITE_INPUT`, `ERR_HYDRO_OUT_OF_DOMAIN`, same semantics as TerrainField):
- `sample_water(x, z)` → in water, kind, channel/body id, water surface, depth;
- `sample_proximity(x, z)` → distance to nearest water and to the major river, nearest id/class, floodplain membership;
- `sample_drainage(x, z)` → catchment/outlet id and contributing area (hydrological fact for R4 moisture; not moisture itself);
- `sample_deformation(x, z)` → hydrology deformation Δ (m).

**HydrologySurface** (`slow_cycle.hydrology_surface/1`) — same API and domain semantics as TerrainField (`sample_height/gradient/normal/lattice`, `get_bounds_m`, `get_region_signature`): height = TerrainField height + Δ exactly; gradient/normal by the identical shared stencil; lattice bit-identical to point calls.

**Initial Alpha constants** (§15 E; final values recorded, later change bumps the schema tag): analysis lattice 32 m; |Δ| ≤ 15 m; major river width 12–60 m; creek width 1–8 m; minimum water-surface slope > 0 along every channel.
