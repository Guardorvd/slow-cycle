**Road deformation (deterministic, order-independent).** Per owner (each piece, connectors included): curvilinear `(s,t)` from bilinear station quads (`p_k + t·n_k` … `p_{k+1} + t·n_{k+1}`; continuous, no projection wedges; valid for |t| ≪ min radius 92.6 m). Road plane `R(s,t) = y(s) − t·tan(bank(s))` for `|t| ≤ a(s) = width/2 + shoulder`, `y(s)` cubic Hermite from design `y` and `grade` (C1). Beyond the plane (lateral or past an end cap) with distance `d` from the plane edge at height `e`: `L = e − 0.5·d` (fill 1:2), `U = e + 1.0·d` (cut 1:1); inside `L = U = R`. Composition:

`F = max(maxᵢ Lᵢ, min(H, minᵢ Uᵢ))`, `H` = HydrologySurface.

max/min is commutative → no request-order dependence, continuous across overlaps, end caps and seams; displacement is exactly zero where the envelopes have daylighted. Diagnostics (counted, never repaired): `ERR_R7_OWNER_CONFLICT` (maxL > minU + 1 mm inside any riding+shoulder plane), `ERR_R7_TIE_UNRESOLVED` (envelope still active at cutoff `a + tie_max + 1 m`), `ERR_R7_INTENT_MISMATCH` (edge delta vs intent > 1 cm or daylight > intent tie + 0.5 m). Attribution for kind/material/collision layer: the active riding plane; ties by stable priority (connector over edge, then piece id).

**Fords.** Use the R6 FORD `y` unchanged. Cells sharing an edge with a fine cell become **transition cells**: a centre vertex fan that includes the fine boundary vertices (no T-junctions). All other cells keep the existing two-triangle convention. Every vertex is a FinalSurface sample at a world-anchored point, so shared vertices are bit-identical across cells and tiles regardless of build order. Normals: fine stencil in fine cells, 16 m stencil on fine-set boundary/fan vertices and coarse cells (lighting only). A road-aligned ribbon was rejected: it needs constrained stitching into the grid and extra overlap rules at patches.
