## 3. Target behaviour

```text
world_seed + coordinate → RegionIdentity → RegionBounds                 (R0, unchanged)
  → MacroGeographyGenerator.generate(identity, bounds) → MacroTerrainPlan (slow_cycle.macro_terrain/2)
  → RegionPlan(identity, bounds, macro)                                 (slow_cycle.region_plan/2)
      MacroTerrainEvaluator (stateless math over the plan) → region_preview (presentation only)
```

Construction (canonical valley frame: u along the valley 0→4096, v across, +v = mountain side; one of 8 exact symmetries maps it to local x,z):

1. **Major valley** — C1 axis spline through 9 points (512 m spacing, bounded meander); floor half-width per point (narrows/opens); one floodplain **basin** widening toward the far side; asymmetric valley walls whose height/width vary along the valley; gentle uplands beyond; floor descends along u.
2. **Main range** — crest polyline `END, SUMMIT, (SADDLE, SUMMIT)*, END` (2–4 summits; each saddle ≥ 60 m below both neighbours = pass). Either spans the region or ends inside it as a massif with a long descending tail. Crest offset from the valley varies (oblique/curved ranges).
3. **Spurs** — 3–6 valley-ward spurs leave the crest (one major), each with 0–2 second-order branches; 1–2 back spurs behind summits. Gaps between spurs form side valleys.
4. **Far side** — a lower meandering ridge over part of the valley length with 1–3 spurs.
5. **Benches** (1–2 valley-wall terraces) and an optional elongated far-side **upland meadow basin** placed beside the far ridge.
6. **Evaluator** — ridge field = smooth-max of concave flank profiles around Catmull-Rom-smoothed crest/spur lines (asymmetric valley/far flank widths, light crest rounding), sampled in a bounded domain-warped frame, masked off the valley floor; added to the valley base; basin flattening; then bounded noise (fBm + ridged on mountains) with valley/bench/basin attenuation: `|final − structure| ≤ amplitude ≤ 6 % relief`.

Exclusions: hydrology/rivers, biomes, rideability, routes/roads, vegetation, FinalSurface, TerrainField/`sample_*` APIs, streaming/LOD/threading, cross-region continuity, player/camera/physics, main-game wiring, MountainMassifField. No gameplay mechanic.
